# Operations Thesis — Holly

How the fund executes — from deal sourcing through close, and the automation layer to keep costs lean at scale.

---

## Operational Philosophy

The fund targets 10–50 unit Class B/C multifamily properties in the $1–5M deal size range. This segment is too small for institutional players but too large for casual individual investors, creating a structural opportunity for an operationally efficient middle-market fund. The operations thesis focuses on:

1. **Lean execution** — Automate repeatable steps (screening, underwriting, due diligence tracking) to reduce per-deal overhead
2. **Market sequencing** — Enter operationally simple markets first, expand as PM infrastructure matures
3. **Deal velocity** — Standardized LOI and DD checklists to compress time-to-close

## Market Entry Sequencing

The market comparison (`RE_Fund_Market_Comparison_HLy.xlsx`) evaluates target markets on:

- **Operational complexity** — landlord-tenant law, licensing requirements, inspection regimes
- **PM availability** — density of qualified property managers for 10–50 unit B/C portfolio
- **Travel logistics** — proximity to team, airport access, drive-time between properties
- **Regulatory burden** — rent control exposure, eviction timelines, local compliance costs

Markets with low operational friction and strong thesis alignment get priority in the deployment schedule.
