## Three Investment Theses

### Thesis 1: The Invisible Supply Wall
Midwest and Northeast secondary markets with structurally frozen construction pipelines. Nationally, Midwest construction is just 3.4% of inventory vs 6% in Sun Belt; starts dropped 74% from 2021 peaks. Class B/C occupancy above 95% in many metros, cap rates of 6.5–9.5%.

**Top picks**: Syracuse NY (9.6% cap, 97.4% occ), Rochester NY (8.3%, #1 competitive), Toledo OH (9.3%, ultra-low entry), Springfield MO (95% occ, 1.1% pipeline), Lexington KY (0.6% pipeline, 7.3% rent growth).

### Thesis 2: The Broken Capital Stack
$310B in multifamily debt matured in 2025, $162B in 2026, $168B in 2027. CMBS delinquency hit 7.15% (March 2026). Sun Belt markets offer 15–30% per-unit discounts from 2022 peaks. Assumable GSE loans at 3–4% create instant equity for buyers.

**Top picks**: Boise ID (vacancy -350bps, 3.3% jobs), Charlotte NC (37.6K jobs #2 nationally), Greenville SC (2.9% job growth, price recovery), Chattanooga TN (pipeline exhausted at 2%), Little Rock AR (zero new starts in 2024).

### Thesis 3: The Hidden Cost Moat
Low-insurance, low-tax inland markets create 150–430 bps of structural operating cost advantage invisible in cap rate comps. Insurance $400–$600/unit in inland markets vs $1,200–$1,800+ in Florida and coastal Texas.

**Top picks**: Boise ID ($2,100–$3,200/yr advantage), Las Vegas NV ($1,500–$3,000, 0% income tax), Cheyenne WY ($1,400–$2,400, 0% income tax), Fayetteville AR ($1,100–$1,700, 94.2% occ), Huntsville AL ($1,500–$2,200, 0.38% tax).

### Cross-Thesis Overlap
**Huntsville AL** appears in all three. **Boise**, **OKC**, **Little Rock**, and **Birmingham** each bridge two theses.
